# Templates index

**Purpose.** Human-owned templates that fix the shape of this repository's agent resources. Open a template when creating the kind of document it shapes, and
not otherwise.

**Not for.** Instance documents. A template defines a shape; the documents that follow it live in their own folders.

**Conventions.**

- These templates are human-owned. Agents may fill in instances; they may not change a template's schema without review.
- Read the relevant template before drafting new context, prompts, skills, or index files.
